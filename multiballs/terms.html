Multiballs Terms of Service

Last updated 27 September 2026.

Multiballs is an unofficial community app for Ballest of Them All. It runs timed Matches on Workshop Maps in the Ballest Discord server, as a bot in its channel and as a Discord Activity. It is made by players, not by the game's developers, and they don't support it.

By using Multiballs you agree to these terms.

Using it

  • Use it as Discord and Steam allow: their own terms still apply to you.
  • Link only a Steam account that is yours.
  • Don't try to break it, overload it, or get around its rules, such as one Match at a time.
  • Times come from Steam's leaderboards. A time that shouldn't count, such as one set with cheats, can be removed, and a Player who sets them can be kept out of Matches.

What we promise, and what we don't

Multiballs is free and comes as it is. It may be down, slow or wrong at times, Matches can be cancelled when Steam or Discord doesn't answer, and it may change or stop at any time. We aren't liable for any loss that comes from using it, as far as the law allows.
